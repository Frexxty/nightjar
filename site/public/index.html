Stratus is a free SoundCloud player for your desktop. Waveforms you can click, comments that scroll with the music, a full-screen mode, and Spotify playlist import.

Waveforms and timed comments

Click the waveform to seek. Watch the comments go by.

Every track shows its SoundCloud waveform, in lists, on its own page, and in the top bar. Click anywhere on it to jump there. Timed comments sit on the waveform as avatars and scroll in a side drawer in time with the song, the way lyrics do in other players. Type in the box at the bottom to post one at the current position.

    Immersive player

    Full screen, with the artwork drifting behind it.

    Press ⇧⌘F and the window turns into a player. The cover art blurs and drifts slowly across the background, the waveform gets big with comment avatars along it, and the right side shows either the live comments or what's up next.

    You can turn the background animation off in Settings.

    Spotify playlist import

    Paste a Spotify playlist link. Get it back on SoundCloud.

    Copy the link to any public Spotify playlist and paste it into Stratus. It looks up every track on SoundCloud and shows you what it found. Check the matches, then save the result as a playlist.

    Stream

    Your SoundCloud stream, reposts and all.

    New posts and reposts from the people you follow, each with its own waveform you can play and seek right in the feed. Like, repost, or send a track to Next Up without leaving the list.

    On your phone

    Install on iPhone.

    The web player is the same app, laid out for a phone: a tab bar, a mini player, and a full-screen now playing view you swipe down to close. Add it to your Home Screen and it opens like an app, with lock-screen controls.

    1. Open stratus-app.k3s.lat in Safari.
    2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
    3. Open Stratus from your Home Screen.

    The rest of what you'd expect.

    • Queue

      Up Next with drag to reorder, remove and clear, plus a History tab. When the queue runs out it keeps going with related tracks, or stops if you'd rather.

    • Your library

      Likes with a filter, playlists, albums, the people you follow, and your listening history.

    • Media keys and Now Playing

      Play, pause and skip from your keyboard, and see the current track in the system's Now Playing controls. Most actions have a shortcut too.

    • Dark, light, or system

      Pick a theme and an accent colour. Orange is the default, but there are others and a custom picker.

    • Demo mode

      Without a backend, Stratus runs on built-in demo data with generated artwork, waveforms, comments and audio, so you can click through everything first.

    • Locked-down window

      The renderer runs with context isolation and the sandbox on, and no Node integration. It can only send playback state and a few window actions to the main process.

    For developers

    Bring your own backend.

    Stratus is only the interface. It never talks to SoundCloud directly. It calls a small REST API that you run, and your server does the SoundCloud part. The contract is 23 routes that return JSON, documented route by route in BACKEND.md.

    Point Settings at your server's URL, add a bearer token if you use one, and press Apply. If you'd rather skip HTTP entirely, implement the Backend interface in TypeScript and plug it in.

    Questions

    macOS says the app can't be opened. What do I do?

    The Mac build isn't signed or notarized yet, so Gatekeeper blocks a normal double-click the first time. In Finder, right-click (or Control-click) Stratus in Applications and choose Open, then click Open again in the dialog. You only have to do this once.

    On recent macOS versions the dialog may not offer Open. In that case, try to open the app once, then go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

    Windows shows "Windows protected your PC".

    That's SmartScreen, which warns about installers that haven't built up a reputation yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

    Is Stratus made by SoundCloud or Spotify?

    No. Stratus is an independent open-source project and isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to SoundCloud or Spotify.

    Do I need SoundCloud Go+?

    It depends on your backend. Stratus plays whatever stream URL your server hands it, so what you can hear in full comes down to the account and API access your backend uses. The app itself has no subscription and costs nothing.

    Can I try it without setting up a server?

    Yes. Out of the box it runs in demo mode with generated tracks, artwork, waveforms and comments. Switch to "HTTP server" in Settings when your backend is ready.
